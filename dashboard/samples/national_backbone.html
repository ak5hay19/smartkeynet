National Backbone — one shared key pool, four agencies
A national quantum backbone serves government agencies from a single shared key pool. One real, held-out episode: each agency's identity and tier, and one agency reclassified mid-operation. Masked DQN (checkpoints/dqn_s1.pt, trained on S1 (steady state), held-out eval) was never trained on this schedule (Hard Rule 8).
Illustrative agency names for this scenario; underlying tenant identities and sensitivity assignment are unchanged from the rest of the system. Not a claim about any real deployment. The tiers were not assigned to fit the story: real tenant-graph seed 30 was found by search because its untouched random assignment already had this shape.

Honest limit: the shared pool did NOT come under pressure in this run -- real pool_fill was 1.00–1.00 of capacity for every decision (S6 has no QKD-degradation mechanism, and changing pool capacity/initial fill in the config had no effect).
