03 — Why this key, not another

There is no narrated reasoning here — the explanation is the pipeline.

1. Threat signal ingestedthreat_score = 0.050
2. Posture classifiedposture = CALM (p = 0.790)
3. Policy floor lookup(S1 x CALM) -> floor = SERVE_CLASSICAL
4. Action mask computed5 of 5 actions legal: SERVE_CLASSICAL, SERVE_PQC, SERVE_HYBRID, REUSE, REKEY_NOW
5. Cost comparison among legal actionscheapest legal option was SERVE_CLASSICAL (latency 1.00, energy 1.00)
6. Final decisionfloor required SERVE_CLASSICAL; several options cleared it and the policy's own learned preference selected REUSE over the cheapest (SERVE_CLASSICAL)
